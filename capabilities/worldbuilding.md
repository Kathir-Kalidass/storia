# Capability: Worldbuilding

## Description
Generates comprehensive fictional worlds including geography, climate, political systems, magic systems, religions, cultures, technology, and ecology. Produces internally consistent world bibles with appropriate scale for the story length and genre.

## Activation Triggers
"build a world", "create a setting", "design world", "worldbuilding", "create a kingdom", "design a magic system", "build a culture"

## Supported Workflows
W005 — Build World

## Preferred Personas
Primary: Fantasy Worldbuilder (or genre-appropriate worldbuilder). Secondary: Civilization Architect, Kingdom Designer, Magic System Designer, Religion Designer, Ecology Designer, Geography Designer.

## Engines Invoked
Runtime Engine — workflow routing. Creative Engine — World Creator, Kingdom Designer, Religion Designer, Magic System Designer, Technology Designer, Culture Creator. Persona Engine — worldbuilding-focused persona composition. Research Engine — historical/cultural research for authenticity. Memory Engine — state persistence of World State.

## Input Requirements
Story Metadata (genre, language, tone for context). May use existing World State for expansion.

## Output
World Bible: geography, climate, ecology, political boundaries, rulers, factions, belief systems, magic rules (if fantasy), technology level, cultural customs, social structures, and economy.

## Constraints
Internal consistency required across all generated systems (magic rules must not contradict technology, ecology must support civilization). Technology anachronisms flagged against world era. Cultural sensitivity review applied. Scale calibrated to story length — epic worlds for epics, focused settings for shorter works.
