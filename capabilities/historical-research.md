# Capability: Historical Research

## Description
Provides contextual research for authentic period storytelling across eras, cultures, and domains. Loads relevant historical, cultural, regional, and professional knowledge when a story references specific time periods or settings.

## Activation Triggers
"research this period", "check historical accuracy", "what was life like in", "verify historical detail", "research culture", "authenticity check"

## Supported Workflows
W002 — Continue Story, W005 — Build World, W006 — Review Chapter

## Preferred Personas
Primary: Historical Researcher. Secondary: Period Accuracy Checker, Medieval Historian, Ancient Civilizations Expert, Alternate History Designer. Cultural: region-appropriate Storyteller persona (e.g., Tamil Storyteller, Japanese Storyteller).

## Engines Invoked
Runtime Engine — research routing. Research Engine — Historical Research, Culture Research, Region Research, Religion Research, Language Research, Profession Research, Weapon Research, Architecture Research, Fashion Research, Food Research. Narrative Intelligence (Verify) — cross-reference research against generated content. Memory Engine — cache research results in archive.

## Input Requirements
Story Metadata (genre, setting, time period). Active location and cultural context from World State. Character backgrounds from Character State. Specific research query or topic.

## Output
Contextual research briefs (period-specific facts, events, daily life details), authenticity guidelines, anachronism warnings, cultural sensitivity notes, and source references. Results cached in archive for future reuse.

## Constraints
Does not modify canonical Story State — research is advisory only. Unresearched contexts flagged with offer to accept or refine. Contradictory research sources presented with both perspectives. Culturally sensitive topics surfaced with caution notes and user decision required.
