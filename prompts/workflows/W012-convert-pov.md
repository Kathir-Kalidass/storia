# W012: Convert POV — Prompt Guidance

## Purpose
Convert a scene or chapter from one character's point of view to another's.

## Critical Rules
1. **Knowledge boundaries**: The new POV character can only know what they have learned or witnessed. Remove any information they wouldn't know.
2. **Voice**: The narration shifts to the new character's voice, vocabulary, and worldview.
3. **Internal state**: Include the new character's emotional reactions, observations, and interpretations.
4. **Plot**: The same events happen, but filtered through a different perspective.

## Constraints
- Plot outcomes must remain the same
- Information the new POV doesn't know must be removed or implied
- Character voice must match established traits in state
