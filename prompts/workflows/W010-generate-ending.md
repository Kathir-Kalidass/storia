# W010: Generate Ending — Prompt Guidance

## Purpose
Write or revise the story's ending — resolve arcs, answer mysteries, deliver emotional payoff.

## Pre-Check
Before writing the ending, verify:
1. All character arcs have resolution (or deliberate open endings)
2. All mysteries have answers
3. All promised payoffs have been delivered
4. The ending matches the established tone

## Ending Types by Genre
- **Literary**: Ambiguous, thematically resonant, character-focused
- **Fantasy**: Epic climax, world changed, heroes transformed
- **Thriller**: Twist or cathartic resolution, loose ends tied
- **Romance**: Emotional reunion, growth acknowledged
- **Mystery**: Reveal, justice, moral complexity

## Constraints
- No deus ex machina (solution must be set up)
- Ending must be satisfying in the genre's terms
- Reader promises from the genre must be fulfilled
