# W007: Fix Continuity — Prompt Guidance

## Purpose
Identify and resolve continuity errors in the Story State.

## Common Continuity Issues
- Character in two locations simultaneously
- Dead character with active fatigue/inventory
- Timeline paradox (event before its cause)
- Knowledge leak (character knows something they haven't learned)
- World contradiction (season, geography, magic rules)

## Process
1. Run full validation scan
2. Examine revision history for when the error was introduced
3. Propose targeted fixes (not rewrites)
4. Verify fix resolves the error
5. Document what was fixed and why
