# W013: Expand Scene — Prompt Guidance

## Purpose
Add detail, depth, or length to an existing scene.

## Expansion Targets
- **Description**: Sensory details, environment, atmosphere
- **Dialogue**: Subtext, beats, pauses, character interaction
- **Internal monologue**: Character thoughts, reactions, decisions
- **Action**: More specific movement, choreography, tension
- **Emotion**: Deeper emotional beats and reactions

## Constraints
- Don't add plot-relevant information that contradicts future events
- Don't change the scene's purpose or outcomes
- Expanded content must match the established tone
