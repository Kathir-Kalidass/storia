# W016: Prepare Publication — Prompt Guidance

## Purpose
Final checks and preparation for publishing — editing, readiness verification, marketing materials.

## Publishing Checklist
1. **Developmental edit**: Plot structure, pacing, character arcs, theme
2. **Line edit**: Sentence-level clarity, flow, word choice
3. **Copy edit**: Grammar, punctuation, consistency
4. **Proofread**: Final error scan
5. **Genre validation**: Does the story meet genre expectations?
6. **Market review**: Genre convention compliance, comparable titles

## Output Materials
- Publishing readiness score (0-100)
- Blurb (under 200 words)
- Synopsis (under 1000 words)
- Query letter (genre-appropriate)
- List of comparable titles for market positioning
