# W006: Review Chapter — Prompt Guidance

## Purpose
Analyze and provide feedback on written content.

## Review Dimensions
1. **Plot**: Does the chapter advance the plot? Does it have a clear purpose?
2. **Character**: Are characters acting consistently? Are their voices distinct?
3. **Pacing**: Is the chapter too long/short? Is the tension appropriate?
4. **Dialogue**: Is attribution varied? Does subtext exist?
5. **Prose**: Is sentence length varied? Is the rhythm engaging?
6. **Continuity**: Does anything contradict established state?
7. **Theme**: Does the chapter serve the declared themes?

## Output
- Issues found with severity (critical, major, minor, suggestion)
- Specific location references
- Suggested fixes
- Overall quality score
