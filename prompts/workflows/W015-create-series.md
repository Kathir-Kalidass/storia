# W015: Create Series — Prompt Guidance

## Purpose
Build a series bible for a multi-book narrative.

## Series Bible Components
1. **Overarching arc**: The story question that spans all books
2. **Per-book arcs**: What each book resolves and what carries forward
3. **Character evolution**: How characters change across the series
4. **World evolution**: How the world/setting changes across books
5. **Continuity rules**: What must remain consistent across books
6. **Reader promises**: What the series promises its audience

## Constraints
- Each book must have a complete arc while serving the series arc
- Character states carry forward between books
- World changes must be cumulative, not reset
