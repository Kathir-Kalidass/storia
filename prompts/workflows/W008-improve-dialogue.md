# W008: Improve Dialogue — Prompt Guidance

## Purpose
Enhance dialogue quality in a specific scene or chapter.

## Focus Areas
1. **Voice**: Does each character sound distinct? Vocabulary, rhythm, dialect?
2. **Subtext**: Are characters saying what they mean, or dancing around it?
3. **Attribution**: Is "said" overused? Are tags varied but not distracting?
4. **Pacing**: Is dialogue too long? Are beats and action穿插 appropriate?
5. **Purpose**: Does the dialogue advance plot, reveal character, or both?

## Constraints
- Preserve existing character voice traits from state
- Don't change the plot outcomes — only improve delivery
- Maintain scene's established tone
