# W014: Compress Scene — Prompt Guidance

## Purpose
Shorten or tighten a scene to its essential beats.

## Compression Targets
- **Adverbs and adjectives**: Remove unnecessary modifiers
- **Redundant description**: Keep only the most vivid detail
- **Overwritten dialogue**: Tighten to essential meaning
- **Internal monologue**: Reduce to key emotional beats
- **Transitions**: Merge or cut scene transitions

## Constraints
- Preserve all plot-critical information
- Maintain character voice (don't strip personality)
- Keep emotional beats intact
- Scene must still flow naturally
