# Workflow W014: Compress Scene

## Intent
Shorten or tighten a scene — remove redundancy, tighten prose, reduce to essential beats.

## Triggers
- "compress", "shorten", "tighten", "condense", "make shorter"

## Pipeline
1. **state-manager** → `load(storyId)` — load state; on failure: stop
2. **state-manager** → `archiveScene(storyId, sceneId)` — backup original; on failure: stop
3. **persona-engine** → `resolve(genre, language, {primary: 'line-editor'})` — load compression persona; on failure: fallback
4. **creative** → `generate('compression', sceneId, targetLength)` — produce compressed scene; on failure: rollback
5. **synthesizer** → `synthesize(compressed, sceneId, state)` — extract changes; on failure: rollback
6. **validator** → `validate(state)` — check no essential facts lost; on failure: flag
7. **diff-engine** → `compute(oldState, newState)` — record compression; on failure: flag
8. **state-manager** → `write(storyId, state, 'scene_compressed')` — persist; on failure: stop
9. **optimizer** → `optimize(state, storyDir)` — compact; on failure: flag

## Output
- Compressed scene
- Original preserved in archive
