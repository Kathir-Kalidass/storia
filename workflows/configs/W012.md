# Workflow W012: Convert POV

## Intent
Convert a scene or chapter from one point of view to another character's perspective.

## Triggers
- "change pov", "change perspective", "convert pov", "from another character"

## Pipeline
1. **state-manager** → `load(storyId)` — load state; on failure: stop
2. **state-manager** → `archiveScene(storyId, sceneId)` — backup original; on failure: stop
3. **persona-engine** → `resolve(genre, language, {primary: 'pov-specialist'})` — load POV persona; on failure: fallback
4. **narrative-intelligence** → `reason(state, 'pov-conversion')` — analyze POV character's knowledge boundaries; on failure: rollback
5. **creative** → `generate('pov-convert', sceneId, targetCharacter)` — produce converted scene; on failure: rollback
6. **synthesizer** → `synthesize(converted, sceneId, state)` — extract changes; on failure: rollback
7. **narrative-intelligence** → `verify(state, converted, 'knowledge-boundary')` — check no info leaks; on failure: flag
8. **validator** → `validate(state)` — consistency check; on failure: flag
9. **diff-engine** → `compute(oldState, newState)` — record changes; on failure: flag
10. **state-manager** → `write(storyId, state, 'pov_converted')` — persist; on failure: stop
11. **optimizer** → `optimize(state, storyDir)` — compact; on failure: flag

## Output
- POV-converted scene
- Knowledge boundary verification
- Original preserved in archive
